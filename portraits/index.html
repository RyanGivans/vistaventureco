The Experience

You do not have to be good at getting your picture taken.

Most people feel awkward in front of a camera. That is normal. A good portrait session should not feel forced, overposed, or uncomfortable.

The session is built around simple direction, natural posing, and finding images that feel polished without making you feel like someone else.

✓ The goal is not to make you look fake. The goal is to help you look confident, natural, and ready to use the images.
